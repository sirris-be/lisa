From 16 to 22 March 2026, European AI Week (EU AI Week) will explore our ability to develop, master and deploy trustworthy AI solutions, aligned with European values and competitive on a global scale. Discover the detailed programme of these open-access events below.

Navigating the security and privacy landscape of modern AI / In the trenches: cybersecurity of AI agents

The goal of this talk is to raise awareness about the underlying principles and challenges in developing secure AI systems, including LLMs, and to review the ongoing research efforts toward stronger mitigations.
