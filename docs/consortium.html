Consortium

The organisations behind the project

LISA combines academic research, industrial knowledge transfer, and public innovation support.

The LISA project is a collaboration of the DistriNet Research Group and Sirris, funded by the VLAIO Collective Research & Development and Collective Knowledge Dissemination / Transfer program. The goal is to accelerate the adoption of research results and practical know-how across a broader group of companies.

Research Partner

DistriNet

DistriNet is a KU Leuven research group embedded in the Department of Computer Science and part of the imec-KU Leuven Security and Privacy Center, with strong expertise in software security, distributed systems, and engineering for security.

Industry Partner

Sirris

Sirris is the collective knowledge centre of the Belgian technology industry. Its Software Engineering Lab supports companies building secure software and turning research into operational improvements.

Funding Partner

VLAIO

The Agency for Innovation and Entrepreneurship supports innovation and entrepreneurship across Flanders and funds initiatives that strengthen technology adoption and collaboration.
